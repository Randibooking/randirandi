# Member and administrator help

Help for members and administrators is at **https://help.randirandi.com/**, in
Norwegian and English: questions and answers, illustrated guides, and a version
for your own agent. It is the destination of the Documentation link in every
RandiRandi page.

For account access, booking changes or refunds, contact your organization using
the address on its sign-in page or booking emails.
