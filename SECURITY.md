# Security reports

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to post@randirandi.com. Include
the affected version or commit, a minimal fictional reproduction, observed
impact and relevant redacted logs. Do not include real member lists,
credentials, authenticator secrets or payment details.

Do not open a public issue containing an exploit or personal data. Claypond
will assess the report and agree on disclosure after a correction is ready.
No fixed response-time or support-period promise is made by this policy.

For ordinary account, booking or refund questions, contact your organization's
administrator. Self-hosted instances are maintained by their own operators.
