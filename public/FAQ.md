# Member and administrator help

For account access, booking changes or refunds, contact your organization using
the address on its sign-in page or booking emails. Its administrators manage
members, availability and booking decisions.

**The sign-in code does not arrive.** The code comes by email and is valid
for 10 minutes. Hotmail, Outlook.com and Live often put it in junk mail: look
there and mark the message as not junk, and the next code reaches your inbox.
If no code arrives at all, contact your organization.

**Signing in with a phone number.** You can enter your phone number, but
RandiRandi sends no text messages: the code goes to the email address your
organization registered for you. If it has none, or the address is wrong,
contact your organization.

administrator to check the payment record if a refund has not arrived.
